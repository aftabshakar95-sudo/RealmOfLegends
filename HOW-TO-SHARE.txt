              HOW TO SHARE REALM OF LEGENDS ONLINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Start Your Application
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open Command Prompt and run:

    cd c:\Users\hp\Desktop\RealmOfLegends\RealmOfLegends.Web
    dotnet run

Wait for: "Now listening on: http://localhost:5138"
KEEP THIS WINDOW OPEN!


STEP 2: Start ngrok Tunnel
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open a NEW Command Prompt and run:

    C:\ngrok\ngrok.exe http 5138

You'll see something like:
    Forwarding    https://abc-xyz-123.ngrok-free.dev → localhost:5138

THAT'S YOUR SHAREABLE LINK!
Copy it and share with anyone.
